# Code of Conduct

Combusta adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that address applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique methods, results, and code, not people.
- Technical disagreement is expected in engineering work; settle it with evidence: equations, references, and reproducible tests.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.
- Do not share confidential or proprietary data belonging to others (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#data-hygiene)).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@alpztel](https://github.com/alpztel). If you prefer not to write publicly, open a private report through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/alpztel/combusta/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer applies the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, depending on the severity and repetition of the behaviour.
